COLOURWAYS

Every colourway is two hexes, body and accent. The other seven slots are derived, so a new colour never needs a redraw.

{{c.label}} {{c.body}}

DERIVING THE RAMP

body       = input
light      = mix(body, white, 45%)
shade      = body × 0.78
outline    = shade × 0.55
accent     = input
accentDark = accent × 0.70
eye, mouth = body × 0.20
cheek      = mix(body, #F472B6, 50%)

RULES

  • Same maths as the coach compositor: mixHex and darkenHex from sprites/compose.ts, so no new colour code.
  • Dark bodies (luminance under 0.18, like Slate, Night, Cocoa) flip the eye to mix(body, white, 88%) and the shine to the body colour.
  • Pick the accent at least 40° of hue away from the body so the bulb never melts into the head.
  • Cosmetics may use @accent and @accentDark, so a beanie or scarf follows the colourway for free.
  • Swatch order in each card: light, body, shade, outline, accent, accentDark, eye.
