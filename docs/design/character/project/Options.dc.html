THREE DIRECTIONS

Same grid, same face, same palette rules. Only the body silhouette changes. Small figures are 24 px and a solid silhouette test.

A · PIP

RECOMMENDED

Egg-dome with one antenna and a pulse bulb. The bulb is the accent slot and can beat with heart rate. A wide flat head takes any hat; nub arms hold items.

B · TUFT

A soft square with two ear tufts tipped in the accent. The sturdiest silhouette and the most room for the face, but the ears clash with most hats and it sits close to Kit and Bun.

C · BLIP

A heart with a face. It says health at a glance, but the notch and the point leave no flat head, hands or feet to hang cosmetics on, and it reads as an icon more than a buddy.
