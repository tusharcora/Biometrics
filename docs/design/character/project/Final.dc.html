SKIN + HAT RULE

A skin is a set of overlay rows (ears, tufts, shells, tops, markings, tails) in ramp roles, plus optional eye positions. It never removes a body cell. Each skin declares headTop (default y 6). Head items shift up by 6 − headTop to sit on wool, a shell, a puff or bumps. If the shifted hat would leave the grid, the skin wins and the hat is hidden. Ears and tufts tuck under hats.
