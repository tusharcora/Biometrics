What actually is HRV?
Nimbus
Think of it as your body's weather gauge. It's how much the gap between heartbeats varies. More variation usually means you're relaxed and ready; a dip often means stress, a hard session or poor sleep is still hanging around. It's most useful against your own normal, not anyone else's.
