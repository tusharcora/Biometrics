Layout A: how should the sentence read?

Same layout, three ways of writing the summary. Each says what happened, why, and what it means for today, in the character's voice (Nimbus here). Bars now show your usual in words too. Low day left, good day right.

What happened → why → what to do

One flowing paragraph that ends with a concrete suggestion for today.

Today: an easy walk rather than a hard workout, and an earlier night.
