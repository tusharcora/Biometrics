Coach cards

These are the final widths (Luna, Mochi, Boba and Jelly at original width, Sprout slimmest, the rest slimmer) with the soft outline. Each card has a collectible number, a focus chip, the character on its own dotted colour panel, a tagline, and a first hello in a speech bubble. Below the cards are two ways the picker could use them. Click the picker you prefer.

How you'd pick one

The current picker shows one character per page with 8 dots. 15 dots at 44pt each won't fit across the screen, so the dots need replacing.

ACard carousel

Swipe through full cards, with the next one peeking in. A strip of tiny sprites replaces the dots, so you can jump straight to anyone. Feels like flipping through a collection.

BGrid + card sheet

All 15 at a glance in a 3-column grid. Tapping one raises its card in a sheet with the hello and the Choose button. Fastest to compare.
