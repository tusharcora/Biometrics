Making "is thinking" feel like the character

Each panel is the coach chat while a reply is on its way, with the coach and its Lightbulb at the 36pt chat size. Click the one you like; they can also be combined (for example A's lines inside B's bubble).

APersonality lines

Each coach has its own thinking lines in its own colour, e.g. "Luna is counting stars…" and "Kit is pretending not to care…". The dots tick in time with the attachment.

BReply placeholder

A reply bubble appears straight away with the coach's name and shimmering lines in its colour. The real answer then streams into the same bubble, so nothing jumps.

CPixel status tag

A small game-style tag in a pixel font with a blinking block cursor, plus the personality line under it. Matches the pixel art the most.
