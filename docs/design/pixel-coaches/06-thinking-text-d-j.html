More ways to show "is thinking"

Seven more ideas, D to J, each in the coach chat at the real 36pt size with the Lightbulb. A to C from the last screen still count. Many of these combine well; for example, D's typing works inside B's bubble. Click every one you like.

DTypewriter

The coach "types" its own personality line letter by letter in its colour, rubs it out, and types the next. Feels like it's really writing to you.

ENameplate

A small coloured name tag sits under the character like a game NPC, switching to "thinking…" while it works. The text belongs to the character rather than the chat.

FWhat it's doing

Shows the steps as it goes ("Reading your sleep" ✓, "Comparing last week" …). The most informative; real steps need the backend to report progress, otherwise they're timed.

GColour shimmer

"Pengu is thinking" in grey with a sweep of the coach's colour passing through it, plus a quiet seconds counter. Modern and calm.

HBouncy letters

The word "thinking" does a little wave, one letter at a time in whole-pixel hops, matching how the character bobs.

IRetro dialog box

An RPG-style text box with a pixel border, the coach's name on a tab and a bouncing ▼. The answer then types out inside it. The most game-like.

JProgress strip

A slim reply card with a coloured progress strip along its top edge and the personality line inside. Sits between B and G.
