Thinking styles: pick which ones to offer

Each style is a small pixel attachment that appears next to the coach while it works on a reply. People choose their favourite in Settings. Every loop ends with an "answer's here" moment: the bulb lights up, the ? turns into !, and so on. Click every style you want in the app.

Choosing one in Settings

  • Where: Settings → Your coach → Thinking style, under the coach row. The preview at the top uses your own coach.
  • Scope: one choice for the whole app. It works with every coach and is kept if you switch coach.
  • Default: Lightbulb.
  • Small sizes: the attachment is drawn in the same sprite pixels as the coach, so it scales with it. Below 24pt it's hidden and the text "… is thinking" carries it instead.
  • Reduce Motion: the attachment shows as a still frame.
